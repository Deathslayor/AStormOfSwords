====================================================================
 NPCs, Jobs, Crafting & Trading - Reference
====================================================================

This file documents the peasant/NPC job system: how jobs are assigned,
what each job produces, and what each job buys/sells. It reflects the
actual behavior implemented in code, not a design target - if a job
says "no crafting", that's because no crafting logic exists for it
yet, not because one was intentionally left out.

Source root:
    src/main/java/net/darkflameproduction/agotmod/entity/custom/npc/
        goals/<job>/...      - the AI goals that do the actual work
        system/<job>/...     - state machines + collection/deposit logic
    src/main/java/net/darkflameproduction/agotmod/block/ModBLocks.java
        - all "<JOB>_BARREL" job block definitions
    src/main/java/net/darkflameproduction/agotmod/util/ItemPricing.java
        - the shared buy/sell price list used by every trading job


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. HOW A PEASANT GETS A JOB
--------------------------------------------------------------------

There is no player-driven job assignment UI. Job assignment is fully
automatic, handled by the town hall:

  - TownHallBlockEntity.assignJobsToUnemployed() periodically scans
    for unoccupied "<JOB>_BARREL" blocks within range of the town
    hall (getJobTypeFromBlock reads the block type to know which job
    it grants).
  - The nearest free job block is handed to the closest unemployed
    ADULT peasant in the town's jobless queue (assignJobToNPC calls
    peasant.setJobType(...) and peasant.setJobBlockPos(...)).
  - Children can never hold a job - Peasant_Entity short-circuits
    hasJob()/getJobType()/setJobType() for child peasants.
  - On assignment, JobSystem.giveJobEquipment() hands out starting
    tools: Farmer -> iron hoe, Miner -> iron pickaxe + 32 torches,
    Lumberjack/Carpenter -> iron axe. Other jobs get no starting gear.
  - Culture (Culture.java / CultureSystem.java - visual appearance
    and name generation) is completely independent of job assignment.
    A peasant's house/culture never influences which job it can get.
  - Right-clicking a peasant opens a dialogue/trading GUI keyed by
    its current job type - this is where players buy/sell (see
    section 2), not a way to assign or change jobs.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. THE SHARED TRADING/ECONOMY SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Grocer, Butcher, Tanner, Tailor, Blacksmith, Carpenter and Trader all
work the same way under the hood: none of them hold their own private
shop stock. Instead they expose the TOWN HALL'S shared item inventory
(TownHallBlockEntity.townInventory) to the player through a per-job
GUI. What each job is allowed to buy/sell, and at what price, is
defined centrally in util/ItemPricing.java:

  - ItemPricing.ITEMS is a map of item -> (base price, job(s) that
    can trade it). Some items are shared between jobs (e.g. cotton
    is both a Grocer and a Tailor item).
  - Player BUYS from the NPC: cost = base price for the six normal
    shop jobs. For the Trader, cost = ceil(base price x 1.25) - a
    25% markup.
  - Player SELLS to the NPC: payout = base price / 2 for the six
    normal shop jobs. For the Trader, payout = ceil(base price x
    0.75) - noticeably better than the 50% every other shop pays.
  - The Trader's catalog is the UNION of every other shop job's
    catalog (isTraderItem() = Grocer or Butcher or Tanner or Tailor
    or Blacksmith or Carpenter). A Trader will buy or sell anything
    any specialist shop would, just at Trader prices.

In short: specialist shops are cheaper to sell to and cheaper to buy
from than the Trader, but the Trader is a one-stop shop for
everything at a worse (but still convenient) rate.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 3. PRODUCTION JOBS (gather or craft raw materials into goods)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Each of these jobs runs on a daily cycle: collect materials from the
town inventory in the morning (if it needs any), work throughout the
day, then deposit whatever it made back into the town inventory once
a day so a shop job can sell it.

FARMER
  Job block:  agotmod:farmer_barrel
  Collects:   nothing - grows its own seed stock.
  Process:    NEEDS_FARM_SETUP -> PLANTING_CROPS -> HARVESTING_CROPS
              -> PATROLLING. Scans a 37x37 area around its barrel for
              farmland, plants whatever crop/seed it already holds,
              and continuously harvests + replants grown crops. No
              fixed input->output ratio - it just farms whatever seed
              type it's carrying.
  Deposits:   every crop and seed it holds once per day - wheat, and
              all AGoT grains/vegetables/berries and their seeds
              (pumpkin, melon, beetroot, carrot, potato, etc).
  Trades:     none directly - the Grocer sells what the Farmer grows.

MINER
  Job block:  agotmod:miner_barrel
  Collects:   nothing.
  Process:    doesn't mine the world directly - every 2 seconds it
              rolls for one item while stood at its barrel. There's
              no fixed loot table any more: on first roll it takes a
              one-time, EXACT census of every recognized ore/stone
              block in every loaded chunk within a 5-chunk radius of
              the job block (unloaded/ungenerated chunks are skipped,
              never force-loaded), using each chunk section's block
              palette for an exact count rather than a sample.
              The census splits into two families - plain rock
              (cobblestone/cobbled_deepslate/gravel) vs. ore
              (everything else: coal, every metal/gem ore, the
              diamond group) - and rolling happens in two steps:
                1. A flat 1-in-20 chance decides whether this roll is
                   even ALLOWED to be an ore at all, regardless of how
                   ore-rich the area is (unless only one family exists
                   nearby at all, in which case that's the only thing
                   it can produce, gate or no gate). This keeps ore
                   feeling special even sitting on a dense vein,
                   instead of flooding the output with ore.
                2. Only once that gate is passed does the real census
                   decide WHICH ore: 50 iron ore blocks vs. 1 gold ore
                   block makes iron 50x more likely than gold that
                   roll, and a type with zero blocks nearby can never
                   come up. The same weighting applies within the rock
                   family too (e.g. mostly-deepslate terrain yields
                   mostly cobbled_deepslate).
              If the census finds nothing minable at all, that roll
              produces nothing.
                Recognized blocks -> yielded item (normal + deepslate
                variants of the same ore count together):
                  stone -> cobblestone, deepslate -> cobbled_deepslate,
                  gravel -> gravel, coal_ore -> coal, iron_ore ->
                  raw_iron, copper_ore -> raw_copper, gold_ore ->
                  raw_gold, lapis_ore -> lapis_lazuli, tin_ore ->
                  raw_tin, silver_ore -> raw_silver, and all 18 other
                  AGoT gemstone ores (amber, amethyst, bloodstone,
                  carnelian, chalcedony, garnet, jade, jasper,
                  malachite, moonstone, onyx, opal, ruby, sapphire,
                  tigers_eye, topaz, tourmaline) each to their
                  matching gem item.
              Diamonds are a special case: there's only one physical
              diamond ore block in the mod (no separate block per
              colour), and its own loot table randomly drops ONE of
              yellow_diamond/transparent_diamond/black_diamond per
              break (vanilla's own weighted loot-pool selection - see
              ModBlockLootTables.diamondsOreDrop). The miner's census
              buckets diamonds_ore/diamonds_deepslate_ore under one
              shared "diamond group" weight matching the block's real
              count, then rolls which of the 3 colours to actually
              give at the moment it spawns the item - so the 3 colours
              come out roughly equally often, weighted overall by how
              much diamond ore is really nearby, never diverging from
              what's really mineable there.
              The census is cached for the job block's lifetime (it
              only re-scans if reassigned to a different barrel) -
              exactly like the Quarry's chunk scan, just over a wider
              area and covering ores as well as stone.
  Deposits:   everything it's produced, once per day.
  Trades:     none directly - ore/ingots are sold by the Blacksmith
              once smelted.

QUARRY
  Job block:  agotmod:quarry_barrel
  Collects:   nothing.
  Process:    stationary, like the Miner - every 2 seconds it rolls
              for one item while stood at its barrel. Like the
              Miner, there's no fixed loot table: on first roll it
              takes an EXACT census (via chunk-section block palette
              counts, not a sample) of the 12 vanilla stone types
              below within the job block's own chunk, and the roll is
              weighted by that real census - a chunk with far more
              granite than diorite yields granite far more often, not
              a flat per-type chance. A quarry on a chunk with only a
              couple of those types present will only ever produce
              those types; if the census finds none of them, it
              produces nothing that cycle.
                stone, cobblestone, deepslate, cobbled_deepslate,
                diorite, andesite, granite, calcite, blackstone,
                basalt, sandstone, red_sandstone
              Once the census picks WHICH type got mined, stone and
              deepslate get one extra step: 2/3 of the time they come
              out as their cobbled form (stone -> cobblestone,
              deepslate -> cobbled_deepslate) and 1/3 of the time stay
              uncobbled, matching how hand-mining normally turns stone
              into cobblestone. No other type has a cobbled
              counterpart, so everything else always yields itself.
              The census is cached for the job block's lifetime (it
              only re-scans if reassigned to a different barrel) -
              same principle as the Miner's radius scan, just over a
              single chunk and limited to stone rather than ores.
  Deposits:   everything it's produced, once per day.
  Trades:     none directly - nothing currently buys raw stone from
              town storage (no stonemason/stone-shop job exists yet).

SMELTER
  Job block:  agotmod:smelter_barrel
  Collects:   up to 15 coal/charcoal from the town inventory each
              morning (this sets its fuel budget for the day).
  Recipe:     1 coal/charcoal smelts up to 8 ore -> 1:1 into ingots.
              Ore is prioritized gold > silver > iron > copper > tin
              when more than one type is available. Max ore processed
              per day = coal collected x 8.
                raw_gold   -> gold_ingot
                raw_silver -> silver_ingot (agotmod)
                raw_iron   -> iron_ingot
                raw_copper -> copper_ingot
                raw_tin    -> tin_ingot (agotmod)
  Deposits:   finished ingots plus any unused coal/charcoal/raw ore,
              once per day.
  Trades:     none directly - the Blacksmith sells the ingots.

LUMBERJACK
  Job block:  agotmod:lumberjack_barrel
  Collects:   nothing - chops real trees in the world.
  Process:    GOING_TO_JOB_BLOCK -> IDLING -> GOING_TO_TREE ->
              CHOPPING -> COLLECTING -> REPLANTING. Searches a
              96-block radius for a tree, flood-fills its connected
              logs and leaves, chops the whole tree, collects the
              drops, then plants the matching sapling on the stump
              if there's dirt/grass to plant into.
  Deposits:   logs and whatever the tree dropped (saplings, sticks,
              apples), once per day (evening).
  Trades:     none directly - the Carpenter turns logs into goods,
              and also sells raw logs itself.

CHARCOAL BURNER
  Job block:  agotmod:charcoal_burner_barrel
  Collects:   up to 128 logs per day from the town inventory (any
              log/wood/stem/hyphae block item).
  Recipe:     1 log burns (every 2 seconds) into:
                90% chance -> 1 charcoal
                10% chance -> 1 charred_log (agotmod)
              Limited to the logs collected that morning.
  Deposits:   charcoal, charred_log, and any leftover unburned logs,
              once per day.
  Trades:     none directly - the Smelter consumes the charcoal.

CARPENTER
  Job block:  agotmod:carpenter_barrel
  Collects:   up to 64 logs + 64 nails (agotmod:nail) + 64 sticks per
              day from the town inventory.
  Recipes:    (one craft per cycle; wood type is auto-detected from
              whatever's in its inventory)
                1 log                          -> 4 planks
              then, keeping at least 4 planks in reserve at all times
              (never lets crafted-goods output outpace planks made):
                2 planks + 2 sticks + 1 nail    -> 1 stool
                1 stool  + 2 sticks + 1 nail    -> 1 chair
                1 chair  + 2 sticks + 1 nail    -> 1 arm_chair
                3 planks + 2 sticks + 1 nail    -> 1 table
                6 planks                        -> 4 stairs
                4 planks + 2 sticks             -> 3 fences
                2 planks + 4 sticks             -> 1 fence_gate
                6 planks                        -> 6 walls
                3 planks                        -> 6 slabs
                6 planks                        -> 3 doors
                6 planks                        -> 2 trapdoors
  Deposits:   every wood product made (plus any leftover saplings),
              once per day.
  Trades:     sells planks, stairs, slabs, fences, gates, walls,
              doors, trapdoors, signs, saplings, logs, stools,
              chairs, arm_chairs, tables, nails and sticks - across
              every wood type in the mod (~70 AGoT wood types).

TANNER
  Job block:  agotmod:tanner_barrel
  Collects:   up to 64 each of rabbit_hide, leather and string from
              the town inventory each morning.
  Recipes:    (one craft per cycle, tried in this order)
                4 rabbit_hide          -> 1 leather
                1 leather + 1 string   -> 1 bundle
                1 leather (only once out of rabbit_hide)
                                       -> 1 boiled_leather (agotmod)
  Deposits:   leather, rabbit_hide, string, bundle and boiled_leather,
              once per day.
  Trades:     sells leather, rabbit_hide, bundle, boiled_leather.

TAILOR
  Job block:  agotmod:tailor_barrel
  Collects:   only flowers from the town inventory each morning (up
              to 64/day, ~70 flower types including all AGoT
              flowers). Cotton, string, wool and leather used below
              are NOT auto-collected - the peasant must already be
              carrying them.
  Recipes:    (one craft per cycle, tried in this order)
                1 cotton (agotmod)            -> 1 string
                4 string                      -> 1 white_wool
                2 white_wool                  -> 1 cloth (agotmod)
                1 flower                      -> 1 matching dye
                  (hardcoded table covering ~70 flowers, e.g. rose ->
                  red_dye, blue_rose -> blue_dye, etc.)
              once per day, additionally:
                5 leather  -> 1 leather_helmet
                8 leather  -> 1 leather_chestplate
                7 leather  -> 1 leather_leggings
                4 leather  -> 1 leather_boots
              (one random armor piece is chosen and crafted per day,
              cost taken from the list above)
  Deposits:   string, white_wool, cloth, fur, any leather armor made,
              and all 16 dye colors, once per day.
  Trades:     sells string, wool, cloth, fur, leather armor, dyes,
              and raw flowers.

ANIMAL HERDERS (Cattle Herder / Chicken Breeder / Pig Breeder /
Sheep Herder)
  Job blocks: agotmod:cattle_herder_barrel, chicken_breeder_barrel,
              pig_breeder_barrel, sheep_herder_barrel
  Collects:   one breeding item per job, up to 64 in stock, requested
              from the town inventory as needed:
                Cattle Herder    -> wheat
                Chicken Breeder  -> wheat_seeds
                Pig Breeder      -> carrot
                Sheep Herder     -> wheat
  Process:    patrols a pen (5-block radius/height around its
              barrel), breeds one adult pair per day when it holds a
              breeding item (consumes 1 item per breeding), and culls
              extra adults past a cap of 10 to keep the pen from
              overflowing. The Sheep Herder additionally shears any
              sheep that's ready. Drops from natural deaths/shearing
              inside the pen are picked up automatically.
  "Crafts":   nothing manufactured - just collects what the animals
              naturally produce:
                Cattle  -> leather, beef
                Chicken -> feather, chicken, egg
                Pig     -> porkchop
                Sheep   -> mutton, wool (all 16 colors)
  Deposits:   the above loot (never the breeding item itself), once
              per day.
  Trades:     none directly - the Butcher/Tanner/Tailor/Grocer sell
              this produce onward.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
 4. PURE SHOP JOBS (no collection or crafting - trade only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

These jobs never produce anything themselves. They just stand at
their barrel and let players buy/sell from the town's shared
inventory (see section 2), filtered to their own item category.

GROCER
  Job block:  agotmod:grocer_barrel
  Sells:      everything the Farmer grows - wheat, eggs, and every
              AGoT vegetable/grain/legume/berry plus their seeds -
              and cotton (shared with the Tailor). Base prices range
              roughly 1-46 coins depending on the item.

BUTCHER
  Job block:  agotmod:butcher_barrel
  Sells:      raw meats only - beef, porkchop, chicken, mutton,
              rabbit, plus ~25 AGoT raw meat variants (sausage, duck,
              goose, venison, bear, mammoth, etc). There is currently
              no raw-meat-to-cooked-meat processing, and no
              hide-to-leather processing on this job - it's a pure
              shop for whatever raw meat ends up in town storage.

BLACKSMITH
  Job block:  agotmod:blacksmith_barrel
  Sells:      the largest catalog in the game - ores, ingots,
              nuggets, tools, weapons, armor, upgrade kits and
              smithing scrolls. Nothing is actively produced by this
              job; it only resells whatever ends up in town storage
              (e.g. ingots from the Smelter).

TRADER
  Job block:  agotmod:trader_barrel
  Sells/buys: the union of every other shop's catalog (Grocer +
              Butcher + Tanner + Tailor + Blacksmith + Carpenter), at
              Trader prices (see section 2: 125% to buy from, 75%
              paid when selling to it - worse to buy from but better
              to sell to than a specialist shop).

GUARD
  Job block:  agotmod:guard_barrel
  No trading, no crafting. Assigned a day/evening/night patrol shift,
  stays within its patrol radius, and fights back against monsters
  and any player who attacks it first. Purely a combat/labor job.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. NOTES FOR FUTURE WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------

- None of the "<JOB>_BARREL" blocks have a crafting recipe in
  ModRecipeProvider.java - there is currently no in-game way to craft
  a job block; they must be placed via creative mode, another datagen
  path, or structure loot.
- CarpenterSystem.doWorkCycle() implements a second, batch-style
  version of the Carpenter's recipe set (and also auto-sells half of
  all planks made) but is DEAD CODE - it's never called from
  anywhere. The live Carpenter logic is entirely in CarpenterGoal.
  If you're touching Carpenter behavior, edit CarpenterGoal, not
  CarpenterSystem.doWorkCycle().
- Butcher, Blacksmith, Grocer and Trader have no dedicated "Goal"
  class at all - they're pure shop-inventory lookups. If you want
  butchers to actually cook meat, or blacksmiths to actually forge
  gear, that logic doesn't exist yet and would need to be added from
  scratch (there's no partial implementation to extend).
